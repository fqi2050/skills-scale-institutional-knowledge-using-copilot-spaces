# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

### Key Interactions
- Work with Product Managers to turn goals and acceptance criteria into implementation decisions
- Partner with Project Managers to align delivery timing, dependencies, and risk communication
- Coordinate with QA and security reviewers to validate readiness before release

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

### Key Interactions
- Work with Developers and UX roles to translate customer needs into feasible, testable solutions
- Partner with Project Managers on timelines, sequencing, and stakeholder communications
- Use data and analytics inputs to assess whether desired outcomes are being achieved

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers to translate business priorities into plans and milestones
- Work with technical leads and delivery teams to monitor execution health and dependency risk
- Coordinate with release and support roles to ensure readiness and smooth transitions to production

---

## UX Researchers & Designers

### Role Summary
UX Researchers and Designers ensure that the user experience is clear, usable, and aligned to customer needs. They help turn product goals into accessible and intuitive workflows.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct user research, interviews, and usability validation
- Define user journeys, pain points, and design requirements
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and interaction patterns
- Collaborate with product and engineering teams to validate feasibility and adoption

### Goals
- Improve customer satisfaction and usability
- Reduce friction in core user flows
- Ensure design decisions are informed by evidence and feedback

### Typical Communication
- Discovery sessions and design reviews
- User research summaries and prototype walkthroughs
- Collaboration in backlog refinement and acceptance discussions

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers to confirm customer needs and priority opportunities
- Work with Developers to turn designs into implementable solutions
- Share feedback with QA and stakeholders to validate whether the experience meets intended outcomes

---

## Technical Leads / Architects

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, architecture decisions, and implementation integrity across the project. They ensure that the solution remains maintainable, scalable, and aligned with business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architectural patterns
- Guide design reviews and implementation trade-offs
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and integration constraints
- Support teams in making consistent, high-quality engineering decisions

### Goals
- Preserve technical quality and long-term maintainability
- Enable effective cross-team delivery and integration
- Reduce rework caused by avoidable technical debt or unclear design decisions

### Typical Communication
- Architecture reviews and technical design discussions
- Engineering planning and dependency alignment meetings
- Risk and mitigation updates during delivery reviews

### Key Interactions
- Work with Product Managers and Project Managers to assess feasibility and sequencing
- Partner with Developers to support implementation quality and consistency
- Coordinate with Security and Release roles to confirm operational and compliance readiness

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate the schedule, readiness checks, communication, and deployment support needed to move features safely into production.

### Responsibilities
- Manage release calendars and deployment windows
- Coordinate validation, rollback planning, and release communications
- Track release readiness across engineering, QA, and support teams
- Help ensure post-release monitoring and escalation paths are clear

### Goals
- Reduce release risk and operational disruption
- Improve release predictability and stakeholder confidence
- Support smooth transitions from delivery to production support

### Typical Communication
- Release readiness check-ins and deployment reviews
- Stakeholder updates during go-live and post-deploy verification
- Escalation notices for blocking or high-risk release issues

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Project Managers and technical leads to confirm milestone and deployment readiness
- Coordinate with QA and support teams to validate deployment quality and user impact
- Work with Product Managers and stakeholders to communicate timing, changes, and known issues

---

## Security & Privacy Partners

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Partners identify and mitigate risk related to confidentiality, integrity, and compliance. They help ensure projects meet required controls and protect customer and business interests.

### Responsibilities
- Review workflows, data usage, and technical designs for security and privacy issues
- Define controls, remediation steps, and required approvals
- Help assess and reduce risks for data handling, access, and incident response
- Support compliance or governance reviews when needed

### Goals
- Minimize risk to the business, users, and data assets
- Ensure compliance with organizational standards and requirements
- Build security and privacy considerations into delivery from the beginning

### Typical Communication
- Security review meetings and design feedback loops
- Risk assessments and remediation tracking
- Incident triage and escalation communication when appropriate

### Key Interactions
- Work with Technical Leads and Developers to review design and implementation choices
- Partner with Product Managers to incorporate compliance constraints into scope and delivery planning
- Provide input to Project Managers and Release Managers so risks are visible before deployment

---

## Data & Analytics Partners

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners help define the metrics, instrumentation, and evidence needed to understand product impact and make informed decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics and measurement strategy
- Identify instrumentation requirements and reporting needs
- Help analyze adoption, quality, performance, and business impact
- Support decision-making with trend and outcome data

### Goals
- Make impact measurable and evidence-based
- Improve decision quality across planning and execution
- Ensure the team learns from outcomes and adjusts with confidence

### Typical Communication
- Metrics planning sessions and dashboard reviews
- Outcome evaluation meetings and post-release analysis
- Data-informed updates to roadmap or backlog decisions

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers to track outcomes against goals and success metrics
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads to ensure data is accurately captured and reported
- Support Project Managers and stakeholders with clear evidence of delivery impact over time

---

## Customer Support & Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Operations representatives bring frontline insight into reliability, service quality, and post-release experience. They help teams understand usage patterns, issues, and support needs.

### Responsibilities
- Surface recurring customer issues and operational pain points
- Support readiness for rollout, adoption, and service handling
- Provide feedback on supportability, documentation, and incident response
- Help prioritize follow-up work based on user impact and service quality

### Goals
- Improve customer experience and operational stability
- Reduce escalations and time-to-resolution for issues
- Maintain readiness for ongoing support and service delivery

### Typical Communication
- Support reviews, incident follow-ups, and release readiness discussions
- Feedback loops on user issues and operational bottlenecks
- Documentation and escalation guidance for support teams

### Key Interactions
- Partner with Product Managers and Project Managers to surface customer-impacting issues and needs
- Work with Developers, QA, and Release Managers to validate readiness and post-release stability
- Support stakeholder communication by highlighting trends, risks, and improvement opportunities

---

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- For cross-functional work, roles may overlap or be shared depending on team size, maturity, and project context.
- The goal is to make ownership, accountability, and collaboration visible so teams can deliver high-quality work with fewer handoff gaps.
